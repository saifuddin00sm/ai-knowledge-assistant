# Northwind Labs — Employee Handbook

*Fictional company. This document was written as demo data for this repository.*

## 1. Working hours and core overlap

Northwind Labs runs on asynchronous work with a daily core overlap window of
10:00–14:00 in each employee's local time zone. Meetings are only scheduled
inside the overlap window. Outside it, nobody is expected to answer messages,
and no response-time target applies.

The standard week is 38 hours. Anything above 45 hours in a single week must be
flagged to the employee's manager, who is required to rebalance the workload in
the following sprint.

## 2. Paid leave

Every full-time employee receives **28 days of paid annual leave** per calendar
year, in addition to public holidays in their country of residence. Leave does
not need to be justified, and requests under five consecutive days are approved
automatically by the scheduling tool.

Up to **five unused days** may be carried into the next calendar year. They
expire on 31 March. Days beyond that cap are forfeited rather than paid out.

New joiners accrue leave from their start date at a rate of 2.33 days per
completed month, and may take leave before it has accrued with manager approval.

## 3. Remote and hybrid work

Northwind Labs is remote-first. Employees choose where they work, with two
constraints:

1. The chosen location must be a country where Northwind Labs has a payroll
   entity or an established contractor arrangement.
2. Stays longer than 30 days in a country outside the employee's country of
   employment must be registered with the People team at least 14 days in
   advance, because they can create tax obligations.

The Berlin and Lisbon offices are open to any employee without booking. Desks in
the Toronto office must be reserved at least one working day ahead.

## 4. Equipment and home office budget

Each employee receives a laptop of their choice from the approved catalogue,
refreshed every three years. On top of that, there is a one-time home office
budget of **EUR 1,200**, which can be spent on a desk, chair, monitor, lighting,
or anything else that improves the workspace. Receipts are required. The budget
resets after five years of continuous employment.

Hardware remains the property of Northwind Labs and must be returned when
employment ends, with the exception of items bought from the home office budget,
which belong to the employee.

## 5. Expenses

Expenses are reimbursed through the finance portal and paid with the next
payroll run after approval. Submissions must arrive within **60 days** of the
purchase date. Items over EUR 500 need manager pre-approval.

Travel is booked in economy class for flights under six hours and premium
economy above six hours. Hotel spend is capped at EUR 220 per night in listed
high-cost cities and EUR 150 per night everywhere else.

## 6. Learning budget

Every employee has an annual learning budget of **EUR 1,500** for courses,
books, and conference tickets. Conference travel is funded separately from the
team's travel budget and does not consume the learning budget. Unused learning
budget does not carry over.

## 7. Performance reviews

Reviews run twice a year, in April and October. Each review combines a written
self-assessment, manager feedback, and two peer reviews chosen by the employee.
Compensation changes are decided only in the April cycle; the October cycle is
feedback-only.

## 8. Parental leave

Northwind Labs offers 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave to all new parents
regardless of gender, on top of any statutory entitlement in the employee's
country. Leave may be split into up to three blocks taken within 24 months of
the birth or adoption.
